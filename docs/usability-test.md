# Testing the directory with the team

The last step of the navigation plan: watch five teammates use the app on their
own phones, and fix wherever they get stuck. About 20 minutes each.

## How to run it

1. Send the person the app's link. Do not show them around first.
2. Read one task at a time. Ask them to think out loud.
3. Do not help. If they are stuck for two minutes, note it and move on.
4. For each task write down: did they finish, how long it took, and where they
   hesitated or went the wrong way.

## The tasks

1. A family in Swannanoa needs food tonight. The mother speaks Spanish. Find a
   place they can go.
2. A dad wants a parenting group for fathers. Find one and save it.
3. A family got a shut-off notice from Duke Energy. Find who can help, and send
   a teammate a link to that program.
4. What free events for toddlers are on this week?
5. A mother needs diapers and has no ID. Find a place, then go back to the list
   you were looking at.

## After five people

List the places two or more people got stuck. Those are the next fixes. Tasks
that everyone finished in under a minute need nothing.
